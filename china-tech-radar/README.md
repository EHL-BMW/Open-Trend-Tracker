# Global EV & NEV Automotive Intelligence Catalog
## Europe Top 500 • China Top 500 • BMW Electrified Fleet (Alle Modelle)

A comprehensive, evidence-backed automotive intelligence platform featuring three complete datasets:
1. **Europe Top 500**: Bestselling Electric Vehicles (BEV) and Plug-in Hybrids (PHEV) across all brands in Europe (EU 27 + EFTA + UK), cross-referenced with the **ADAC Autokatalog** and enriched with *Preisliste* equipment and ADAS data.
2. **China Top 500 (华系纯自主品牌)**: Bestselling New Energy Vehicles (BEV, PHEV, EREV) strictly limited to **Chinese domestic brands only** (*纯自主品牌*), cross-referenced with **懂车帝 (Dongchedi)** retail sales rankings, **乘联会 (CPCA)**, and **汽车之家 (Autohome)** configuration parameters (800V SiC, LiDAR counts, Huawei ADS 3.0 / XPeng XNGP / Li Auto AD Max, HarmonyOS / HyperOS / 8295 chips, prices in 万元 RMB and EUR).
3. **BMW Electrified Fleet (53 Modelle)**: Vollständige Erfassung **aller 53 existierenden** rein elektrischen (BEV) und Plug-in-Hybrid (PHEV) Modelle von BMW (iX1, iX2, iX3, i4, i5, i7, iX, 2er, 3er, 5er, 7er, X1, X2, X3, X5, XM, M5, i3, i8) mit offiziellen Preislisten-Ausstattungen (*BMW Curved Display, Interaction Bar, Bowers & Wilkins, Luftfederung, Anhängelasten bis 2.700 kg, ADAC Autokatalog Links*).
