PHASE 04

Mathematical Modeling & Analytics (Likes vs. Friction)

Net Sentiment & Severity Formula

To make customer sentiment objectively quantifiable, we formulated a rigorous mathematical model. It cleanly separates positive customer praise (Likes) from real dissatisfaction (Friction) and weights complaint severity according to automotive FMEA principles.

1. Net Sentiment Score (%):
Net = [ ( Likes - Friction ) / ( Likes + Friction ) ] * 100%

Scale from +100% to -100%. Positive = Net customer advocacy; Negative = Friction predominates.
2. Average Severity Score:
Severity = [ 3*Defect + 2*DiffToUse + 1*Wants ] / Friction

Weights: Defect = 3 (Hardware), Difficult to Use = 2 (UX), Wants = 1 (Wish). Scale 1.00 – 3.00.
3. Priority Backlog Index:
Prio Index = Friction_Volume * Severity

Multiplicative priority index for the engineering pipeline to pinpoint the highest-leverage fixes.

💡 The Fundamental Paradox of the BMW Customer Study:
• Hardware & Mechanics are World-Class: With 9.95/10 satisfaction and +44.7% Net Sentiment, chassis engineering on i5 and i7 represents the gold standard.
• The Achilles Heel is Software & Ergonomics: Over 53% of all complaints are not hardware breakdowns (`Defect`), but `Difficult to Use` (UX friction). The vehicles are mechanically sound – customers are frustrated by nested touchscreen menus, oversensitive door sensors, and hidden battery thermal preconditioning toggles!
